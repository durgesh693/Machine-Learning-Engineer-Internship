## 🏢 Business Overview & Context

In the competitive athletic apparel and footwear industry, maximizing operating margins while balancing wholesale distribution and direct-to-consumer (D2C) channels is critical for sustainable growth. 

* **Primary Objective:** Deliver an interactive executive decision support dashboard for C-suite and commercial leadership to track gross turnover, profit margins, geographic distribution, and fulfillment efficiencies.
* **Dataset Scope:** 9,648 verified retail sales records across multi-tier retail channels in the United States.
* **Core Tooling:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), Power Query, Microsoft Excel.
